Draw real employees from the test set (people the model never saw during training) and compare, for each group, the share who actually earn more than $50K with the share the model expects from their job profile.

Unexplained gap = actual share of high earners − share expected from the job profile, in percentage points. A negative gap means the group earns less than its profile predicts. It is significant when its 95% confidence interval does not cross 0. It is a reason to investigate, not a proof of discrimination.
